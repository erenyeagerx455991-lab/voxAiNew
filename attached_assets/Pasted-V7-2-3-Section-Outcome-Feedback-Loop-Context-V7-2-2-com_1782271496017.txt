V7.2.3 — Section Outcome Feedback Loop

Context

V7.2.2 complete.

Verified active:

- Design DNA System
- Design RAG
- Section-Level Design RAG
- 260+ Section References
- Design Evaluator
- Auto Repair Loop
- Multi-Candidate Selection
- Benchmark Framework
- Component Intelligence

Current limitation:

Section references are retrieved.

Section references are used.

Section references are tracked.

But section references are NOT learning from outcomes.

Current flow:

Section Reference
↓
Generation
↓
Evaluation
↓
Repair
↓
End

Missing:

Outcome Learning

Result:

High-performing hero references are not promoted.

Weak pricing references are not demoted.

System is retrieval-aware but not outcome-aware.

---

Goal

Create a self-improving section retrieval system.

References that consistently produce:

- higher evaluator scores
- fewer repairs
- stronger accessibility
- stronger conversion structure

must automatically rank higher over time.

Poor references must automatically rank lower.

---

PHASE 3 — Quality Score Formula

Create:

calculateSectionQuality()

Formula:

qualityScore =

overallScore × 0.40
+
accessibilityScore × 0.20
+
layoutScore × 0.15
+
ctaScore × 0.10
+
heroScore × 0.10
+
(1 - repairRate) × 0.05

Normalize:

0–10

Rules:

New references:

Default score = 5

Avoid cold-start bias.

---

PHASE 4 — Retriever Upgrade

Upgrade:

sectionRetriever.ts

Current:

DNA Match
+
Industry Match
+
Design Match

New:

DNA Match
+
Industry Match
+
Design Match
+
Section Quality Score

Weight:

Section Quality Score

max contribution = 20%

Never allow quality score to overpower DNA matching.

DNA remains dominant.

---

PHASE 5 — Auto Promotion

Promote references when:

usageCount >= 5

AND

avgScore >= 9

AND

repairRate < 15%

Bonus:

+1.5 retrieval points

Promotion must be reversible.

---

PHASE 6 — Auto Demotion

Demote references when:

usageCount >= 5

AND

avgScore < 7

OR

repairRate > 50%

Penalty:

-2 retrieval points

Demotion reversible.
