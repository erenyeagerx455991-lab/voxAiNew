V8.2 — AI UX Intelligence & Conversion Prediction Engine

OBJECTIVE

Build the complete V8.2 UX Intelligence & Conversion Prediction Engine.

This is a new architecture layer that sits after V8.1 Self-Evolving Design DNA and before the Design Evaluator.

The purpose of V8.2 is to predict how well a generated UI will perform from a UX and conversion perspective before it is shown to the user.

This engine must never replace existing systems.

It must only enhance them.
