V9.4 — Autonomous Knowledge Intelligence Engine

Objective

Implement the complete V9.4 Autonomous Knowledge Intelligence Engine.

This engine becomes the long-term intelligence layer of VoxAI.

Its responsibility is NOT to generate UI, code, or architecture.

Its responsibility is to continuously accumulate, organize, retrieve, rank, and evolve knowledge across every project, every build, and every learning event.

The engine transforms VoxAI from a request-driven AI into a knowledge-driven autonomous software engineering platform.
