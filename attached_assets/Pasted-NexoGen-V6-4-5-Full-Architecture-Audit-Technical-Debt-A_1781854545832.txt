NexoGen V6.4.5 — Full Architecture Audit & Technical Debt Analysis

Objective

Perform a complete architecture audit of the NexoGen codebase.

This is NOT a feature release.

Do NOT add new UI.

Do NOT redesign workflows.

Focus only on identifying architectural weaknesses, technical debt, scalability risks, maintainability issues, security gaps, performance bottlenecks, and future blockers.
