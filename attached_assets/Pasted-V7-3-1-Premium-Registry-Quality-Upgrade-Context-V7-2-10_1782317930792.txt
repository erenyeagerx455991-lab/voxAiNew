Phase 2 — Hero Quality Upgrade

Review all hero templates.

- Eyebrow badge
- Strong headline
- Supporting copy
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Trust signal
- Visual asset zone

- dual CTA
- trust signal
- hierarchy

Phase 3 — CTA Optimization

Review every CTA section.

Enforce:

- One dominant action
- One secondary action
- Benefit-driven copy
- Urgency where appropriate

Disallow:

- Generic "Learn More"
- Generic "Get Started"

Prefer:

- Start Free Trial
- Book Demo
- Create Workspace
- Launch Project

Phase 4 — Trust Layer Upgrade

Review:

- Testimonials
- Logo clouds
- Social proof
- Statistics

Require at least one:

- customer quote
- review score
- company logos
- success metric

per trust section.
