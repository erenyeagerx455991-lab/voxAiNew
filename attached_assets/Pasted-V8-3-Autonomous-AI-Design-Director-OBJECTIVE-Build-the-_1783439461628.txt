V8.3 — Autonomous AI Design Director

OBJECTIVE

Build the complete V8.3 Autonomous AI Design Director.

This is a new intelligence layer that sits after UX Intelligence (V8.2) and before Candidate Selection.

The Design Director acts like a 20+ years Senior Product Designer + Creative Director + UX Director.

It does NOT generate React code.

It does NOT replace the Design Critic.

It does NOT replace the Evaluator.

Instead, it performs high-level strategic design review before the final build is selected.

Its responsibility is to answer one question:

«"If this website were reviewed by a world-class design team, what would they improve before shipping it?"»
